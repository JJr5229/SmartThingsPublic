## What SPPS already has

You are ahead of most districts. SPPS runs its own AI professional development —
*AI Tools: Gemini & Notebook LM* (#90819), *Generative AI for Educators* (#90791), and
*AI Tools: Canva Magic Studio* (#90820) — and district staff meet regularly to guide
responsible AI use. Google offers additional free training, including a certificate
course teachers can submit for PD credit.

**Staff should take all of it. This proposal does not replace any of it.** Google's free
course is a prerequisite for this series, not a competitor to it.

## The gap

**Almost all Google AI training is written for Chromebooks. SPPS runs iPads.**

With roughly 40,000 iPads in a 1:1 program and 4,000+ MacBooks for instructional staff,
SPPS staff are being handed training that assumes a device they do not have — desktop
Chrome, right-click menus, browser extensions, a side panel that is somewhere else or
absent on an iPad. Staff hit friction that the training does not acknowledge, and many
conclude the tools do not work rather than that the instructions were written for
someone else's hardware.

**And most national AI training covers FERPA and stops.** Minnesota educational data is
governed by **Minn. Stat. § 13.32** under the Minnesota Government Data Practices Act — a
broader regime. Staff trained nationally have an incomplete picture of their obligations
here.

## What this series is

Eight modules, ~11.5 contact hours, that begin where the free courses end.

| # | Module | Artifact staff leave with |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Foundations & the SPPS Landscape | Tool-boundary map |
| 02 | Prompting Essentials (PTCF) | 5-prompt library |
| 03 | Gemini Across Workspace — **iPad-first** | 3 weekly workflows |
| 04 | NotebookLM as a Grounded Curriculum Library | Course notebook + Audio Overview |
| 05 | Gemini in Classroom & **Building Gems** | A working custom Gem |
| 06 | Privacy, Ethics & **Minnesota Law** | Team data-handling rule |
| 07 | Teaching Students With and About AI | Redesigned AI-resilient assessment |
| 08 | Capstone — Role Tracks & Toolkit | Published toolkit + peer demo |

**Four things this does that free training does not:**

1. **iPad-first.** Every entry point verified and screenshotted on the devices SPPS runs.
2. **Minnesota law.** Built on § 13.32 and MDE guidance, not FERPA alone.
3. **Depth past the demo.** Gems loaded with a teacher's real pacing guides and rubrics —
   the highest-leverage feature in the Google stack and the least taught anywhere.
4. **Artifacts, not awareness.** Eight built things that stay in the account after the
   session ends.

## What we would need from SPPS

- **Confirmation of your Workspace for Education edition and which AI features are
  enabled** — this determines what can be taught
- Your current AI guidance for staff and students
- **A district representative to attend or co-present Module 06** — this is professional
  learning, not legal advice, and district policy governs
- Participants complete Google's free prerequisite course first

## The proposal: one paid pilot cohort

Not a district rollout. One cohort, evaluated honestly.

- **12–18 participants**, mixed roles, volunteers — including at least one administrator
  and one support-staff member
- **Eight weekly 90-minute sessions**, or four half-days
- **A 30-day follow-up included**, where practice is verified rather than assumed
- **An evaluation report** covering what worked, what did not, and what your license
  would need to enable

You are invited to the Module 08 peer demonstrations. Judge the series by what staff can
actually do at the end of it.

## What this is not

- Not a Google certification — Google's own free courses issue those, and staff should
  take them
- Not legal advice, and not a substitute for district policy
- Not coverage of Microsoft Copilot, Canva Magic Studio, or third-party AI tools
- Not permanent: Google changed these products materially during 2026 alone, and the
  material requires annual refresh. The durable skills — prompting structure, source
  grounding, de-identification, assessment design — transfer to whatever ships next.
