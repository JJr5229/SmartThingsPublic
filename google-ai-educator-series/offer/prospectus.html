The finding this series is built on

Almost every Google AI course for educators is written for Chromebooks. SPPS runs iPads.

Reporting on SPPS Technology Services describes one of the Midwest's largest and longest-running 1:1 iPad programs. Meanwhile the free training staff are pointed toward — Google's own courses, the Educator Series, most conference PD — assumes ChromeOS, desktop Chrome, right-click menus, and browser extensions. On a district iPad, much of that instruction is unfollowable. Staff hit friction the training never mentions, and conclude the tools don't work rather than that the manual was written for someone else's hardware.

Positioning

The district already teaches the intro. This starts where it ends.

SPPS runs its own AI professional development, and Google gives an equivalent course away free with a PD-creditable certificate. Selling a beginner course here would mean competing with two free incumbents and losing — so the free course is a prerequisite for this series, not a competitor to it.

Already available, free

  • SPPS #90819 — AI Tools: Gemini & Notebook LM
  • SPPS #90791 — Generative AI for Educators
  • SPPS #90820 — AI Tools: Canva Magic Studio
  • Google — Generative AI for Educators with Gemini, ~2 hrs, certificate
  • Google + ISTE+ASCD — free Gemini training pledged to ~6M U.S. educators

Staff should take all of it. Required before Module 01.

What no one has built for SPPS

  • iPad-first instruction — every entry point verified and screenshotted on the devices staff hold
  • Minnesota law — Minn. Stat. § 13.32 under the MGDPA, broader than FERPA, plus MDE guidance
  • Depth past the demo — Gems loaded with a teacher's real pacing guides, rubrics and anchor texts
  • Artifacts, not awareness — eight built things that stay in the account after the session ends

Method

Four commitments

Describe the class, not the child

De-identification is drilled, not lectured. Every workflow carries a named review step and an explicit "never include" line before it leaves the room.

Source grounding over guesswork

NotebookLM answers only from uploaded sources and cites the passage. Staff learn when accuracy about their materials requires it — and where it still needs review.

Partner tests, not seat time

A prompt a colleague can run. A workflow a colleague could adopt. An assessment a colleague can't break with one prompt. Harder to fake than a completion box.

Honest about limits

AI detection is unreliable and flags English learners disproportionately. Gemini in Classroom is English-only in a multilingual district. Both are named, not glossed.

Before anything is promised

Five questions that reshape the series

  1. Which Workspace for Education edition does SPPS hold — Fundamentals, Standard, Teaching & Learning add-on, or Education Plus? Any Google AI Pro for Education?
  2. Which AI features are actually enabled in the Admin console? Licensed is not the same as switched on. This determines what can be taught at all.
  3. Who is the district AI guidance group? SPPS states that administrators and district-level staff meet regularly on responsible AI use. They are the buyer or the gatekeeper.
  4. What do #90819, #90791 and #90820 already cover? The syllabi keep this series from repeating them.
  5. Is Gemini enabled for under-18 students, and what is the district's position on the English-only limit?

Module 03 depends most heavily on licensing: Gemini in Docs, Sheets, Slides, Vids and Forms is tied to Education Plus or a Teaching & Learning add-on. Gemini in Classroom is free across all editions, which makes Module 05 the safest high-value session regardless of how these resolve.

Engagement shape

One paid pilot, evaluated honestly

Not a district rollout from a cold proposal. Districts buy pilots far more readily than programs, and a pilot that works sells itself internally.

Phase 0

Discovery

The five questions above, answered before anything is offered or priced.

Phase 1

One cohort

12–18 volunteers, mixed roles, including at least one administrator and one support-staff member. One deliberate skeptic.

Phase 2

30-day follow-up

Included, not an add-on. Which workflows are still running, and what broke. This is where the evaluation data comes from.

Phase 3

Report & scale

Artifact-use audit by role, hours saved, and what failed. Then train-the-trainer, so the district can run it internally.

Verification status. Every SPPS-specific finding on this page — device counts, PowerSchool course numbers, the district AI guidance group — was reached through search summaries, not by reading the district's own pages, which were unreachable from the drafting environment. Treat them as research leads to confirm with SPPS Technology Services before this is sent.

Scope. Professional learning, not legal advice; district policy governs, and a district representative should co-present Module 06. This series confers no Google certification — Google's free courses issue those, and staff should take them. It does not cover Microsoft Copilot, Canva Magic Studio, or third-party education AI tools.

Shelf life. Google changed its education AI products materially during 2026 alone. Feature claims require re-verification before each delivery cycle; the durable skills — prompt structure, source grounding, de-identification, assessment design — transfer to whatever ships next.
