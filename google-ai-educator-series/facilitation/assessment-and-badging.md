# Assessment & Badging — evidencing completion for PD credit

SPPS registers staff PD through **PowerSchool** (existing AI courses run as sections
#90819, #90791, #90820). Any series offered to the district must produce completion
evidence that fits that system, and must fit Minnesota's requirements for relicensure
clock hours.

⚠️ **Confirm with SPPS Human Resources and the PD office:** what evidence they require,
who approves outside providers for clock hours, and whether the series can be registered
as PowerSchool sections. This is an administrative gate that can stop an otherwise
successful pitch — resolve it during discovery, not after.

---

## The principle: assess artifacts, not attendance

Seat time is the weakest possible evidence and districts know it. Every module in this
series produces a concrete artifact, which makes genuine competency evidence available
at no extra cost.

| Module | Evidence of completion |
|---|---|
| 01 | Completed tool-boundary map; correct answers on the two scenarios |
| 02 | Five PTCF prompts that a *partner* could run successfully |
| 03 | Three workflows with named review steps and time-saved estimates |
| 04 | A functioning notebook with real sources + one Audio Overview placed in a sequence |
| 05 | A Gem that produces useful output, with documented refinements |
| 06 | Team data rule + correct classification on the five scenarios |
| 07 | A redesigned assessment that survived the partner stress test |
| 08 | Published toolkit + peer demonstration |

**The partner tests are the real assessment.** A prompt a colleague can run, a workflow a
colleague could adopt, an assessment a colleague could not break with one prompt — these
are meaningfully harder to fake than a completion checkbox, and they are what a director
would want to see.

---

## Three completion tiers

Offering tiers lets participants who cannot commit fully still get credit for real work,
which materially improves enrollment.

**Participant** — attended 6 of 8 modules, completed 6 artifacts.
*Evidences: exposure and basic capability.*

**Practitioner** — all 8 modules, all 8 artifacts, delivered the Module 08 demonstration,
**and** attended the 30-day follow-up with evidence that at least two workflows are still
in use.
*Evidences: sustained practice. This is the meaningful credential.*

**Lead** — Practitioner, plus the Module 08 Track D coaching plan, plus documented
delivery of a share-out to colleagues in their building.
*Evidences: capacity to spread the practice. This tier is the district's path to scale
without buying more delivery.*

The 30-day requirement for Practitioner is deliberate and should not be negotiated away.
It is the difference between certifying that someone sat through training and certifying
that they changed their practice.

---

## The 30-day follow-up

**Part of the engagement, not an add-on.** Ninety minutes.

Agenda:
1. **What's still running?** Workflow by workflow, honestly. (20 min)
2. **What broke?** The most valuable segment — and the primary input for revising the
   series. (25 min)
3. **Troubleshooting clinic** — fix the broken ones live. (25 min)
4. **Re-verify** — what changed in the products since the series ran? Something will
   have. (10 min)
5. **Practitioner sign-off** and next commitments. (10 min)

---

## Evidence package for the district

Delivered after the follow-up, per cohort:

- Completion roster by tier, formatted for PowerSchool entry
- **The artifact audit** — how many of eight artifacts are in active use at 30 days,
  broken out by role. The single most useful number in the report.
- Self-reported hours saved per week, by role, with the caveat that it is self-reported
- **What did not work** — modules that landed poorly, features that were not enabled,
  content that needs revision
- Recommended next steps

**Report the failures.** A report claiming every module landed perfectly is not credible
and districts discount it. A report saying "Module 03 partially failed because three
Workspace AI features are not enabled on our license — here is the revised version and
here is what Technology Services would need to enable" is worth far more, and it is the
kind of report that gets you invited back.

---

## What this does not do

Be honest in any proposal:

- This series does **not** confer a Google certification. Google's own free courses issue
  certificates that staff can submit for PD credit. **Encourage participants to take
  those too** — they are the prerequisite, they are free, and recommending a competitor's
  free product builds more trust than withholding it.
- It does **not** substitute for district policy training or legal guidance.
- It does **not** cover Microsoft Copilot, Canva Magic Studio (#90820), or third-party
  education AI tools.
