Who we are

We listen, we care.

Seated at a kitchen table in 1990, Jeanette Mefford and Sally Knutson started Mefford, Knutson & Associates with a single goal in mind: to strategically position home health and community-based businesses for success in a transitional marketplace.

Thirty-five years later, that is still the work. We offer a blend of comprehensive and cost-effective organizational development and redesign services for small to mid-size businesses that deliver home health and community-based care — plus the consulting, training and tools to meet the unique needs of each client at every stage of a home care based business.

What we do

Consulting, training and tools — at every stage

Whether you are opening your doors, adding a license type or preparing for your next survey, we meet you where you are.

Customized consultation

Highly customized consultation for new and existing home care services. Bring us the situation in front of you and we will work it through with you — available by the hour, no long engagement required.

Policy development

Administration, client care, personnel and infection control — written to the requirements that actually apply to your license type, not a generic template.

Training

Training for the people who have to live inside the policies every day — including home health aide competency testing materials with the test, answer key and certificate of completion.

Organizational development & redesign

A comprehensive and cost-effective blend of organizational development and redesign services, built for the small to mid-size businesses that actually deliver home health and community-based care.

Licensure application support

We will walk you through the application — and for a 245D license, once your initial steps are complete, we can prepare and submit the application on your behalf.

Forms & documentation systems

Charting tools from admission through discharge, personnel and hiring records, service agreements and consents — the paper trail a surveyor asks for, already built.
