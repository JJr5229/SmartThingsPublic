Common questions

Before you call

It depends on the services you provide. A basic license covers assistance with most activities of daily living in a person’s home. If you provide skilled services — nursing, assessments, delegation, medication administration — you need a comprehensive license, and a registered nurse must be named on the application. Our license finder will point you to the likely path in two questions.

A license is generally issued within ninety days of a completed application — the qualifier that catches most new agencies. New home care licenses are issued as temporary for one year, and convert to permanent following a successful compliance survey.

Not while your license is still temporary. Medicare certification requires a comprehensive home care license, and you cannot apply for certification until the permanent license is in place. Plan your timeline accordingly.
