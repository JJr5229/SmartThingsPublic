About MKA

It started at a kitchen table.

Thirty-five years later, Mefford, Knutson & Associates is still doing the same thing — helping Minnesota home care providers do this work right.

Our story

A single goal, held for thirty-five years

While seated at a kitchen table in 1990, MKA founders Jeanette Mefford and Sally Knutson started Mefford, Knutson & Associates with a single goal in mind: to strategically position home health and community-based businesses for success in today’s transitional marketplace.

The marketplace has been transitional ever since. Minnesota consolidated assisted living under a single facility license in 2021. Home care licensure has been reshaped around it. Requirements move every legislative session, and the providers who come through it intact are the ones whose policies, forms and records were already right.

We offer a unique blend of comprehensive and cost-effective organizational development and redesign services for small to mid-size businesses that deliver home health and community-based care. In plainer language: we help small agencies carry a compliance burden that was written for large ones.

Mefford, Knutson & Associates brings a combined sixty years of experience with up-to-date knowledge of all critical requirements that will help your business succeed.
