02 What the audit found

I crawled all 83 URLs on ezplumbingmn.com and loaded the homepage in a real browser on a simulated phone. Everything below was measured, not guessed.

Critical

Every page shows a phone number that isn't yours

All 83 pages display 612.392.0832 as text, while the tap-to-call link underneath is tel:651-392-0832. Your Google profile says (651) 392-0832 — so the link is right and the number people read and dial is wrong. Anyone on a desktop, or anyone who reads it off the screen and dials from another phone, gets a wrong number. It also means your website and your Google listing disagree about how to reach you on 83 pages, which is a signal Google uses when it decides who to show in local results.

Critical

Four links on your homepage say "Button"

In the services grid, visible to everyone. Two also go somewhere odd — the emergency-repair card links to your About page, and "Customized Plumbing Solutions" links to water filtration. A homeowner deciding whether to let a stranger into their house at midnight is reading that page for signs you're careful.

High

Your business details aren't in the site's code anywhere

No address, no hours, no license number, and no LocalBusiness markup on any of the 83 pages — the machine-readable block that tells Google who and where you are. The only markup on the site is a four-line stub with your name and URL in it. Your 4.9 rating and 216 reviews don't appear on the website either.

High

3.3 MB and 50 round trips to load your homepage on a phone

1.9 MB of that is JavaScript that doesn't do anything a visitor would notice — it's the page builder's machinery. On office wifi this is invisible. In a basement with one bar, which is where your emergency customers actually are, it isn't.

Already good

Your 74 city pages are genuinely well done — don't let anyone rewrite them

I hashed the text of all 83 pages to check: every city page is unique, roughly 1,870 words, individually titled, with FAQ markup. Zero broken links across the whole site. Your security headers score 4 out of 6, which beats most small-business sites. That work is an asset and the rebuild carries it forward rather than replacing it.

Requests — each one is a separate trip your visitor's phone has to make just to show the page. Fifty trips versus four. Fewer trips means less waiting on a weak signal.

Page weight — how much has to come down the pipe before anything is usable. 3.3 MB is roughly a three-minute song. 71 KB is a couple of text messages.

JavaScript — code the phone has to download and then run, so it costs twice: once to arrive, once to execute. Nearly two megabytes of it on your site is doing nothing you'd miss.

Security headers — instructions your server gives the browser about how to protect the visitor. Yours does most of this right already; the rebuild covers the last two.

LocalBusiness markup — a hidden block of code stating your name, address, phone, hours and service area in a format Google reads directly instead of guessing at. It's how you tell Google you're a plumber in Blaine who covers 74 cities. Right now the site doesn't say it anywhere.

I ran this signed out, so this covers the 16 factors anyone can verify from the public listing. It is not the 22-factor score and I'm not going to present it as one. Owner access closes the gap.

Three findings worth acting on

  • Your website contradicts your profile. Same phone number problem as section 02, and this is the side where it costs you rankings rather than just calls. Google leans on your website to corroborate your listing; right now 83 pages disagree with it. The fix is on the website — your profile is the one that's right.
  • A directory has you under a different name in a different city. There's a Nextdoor page for this business filed as "EZ Sewer & Drains" in Dayton, MN, against a profile that says "EZ Plumbing & Drains" in Blaine, MN. Mismatched name-and-city pairs are among the more damaging citation problems, and where one exists there are usually several.
  • Your profile links to your homepage and nothing else. Someone searching "plumber in Coon Rapids" finds your profile and lands on a generic homepage — while your 1,870-word Coon Rapids page sits there unlinked. There's also no tracking on the link, so there's currently no way to tell how much business the profile actually sends you.

For balance: 4.9 stars from 216 reviews is an excellent profile, your hours and booking link are set up correctly, your attributes are filled in, and your July post about the copper water-line repair is genuinely well written. Posting has just gone quiet — that one is 28 days old.
