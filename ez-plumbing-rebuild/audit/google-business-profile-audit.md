## Findings

### G1 — The website contradicts the profile's phone number (critical)

The profile publishes **(651) 392-0832**. The website displays **612.392.0832** as text on
all 83 pages, while linking `tel:651-392-0832` underneath.

This is the same defect as C1 in the website audit, and it is listed again here because
the damage is different on this side. Google treats the phone number on your website as
corroborating evidence for the number on your profile. A mismatch across 83 pages weakens
the strongest consistency signal you control, and it is the sort of thing that quietly
suppresses local pack placement without ever producing an error message.

**Fix:** correct the website. The profile is the one that is right.

### G2 — A directory lists the business under a different name and city (high)

A Nextdoor business page for this company resolves at the slug
`ez-sewer-drains-dayton-mn` — that is **"EZ Sewer & Drains"** in **Dayton, MN**, against a
profile that says **"EZ Plumbing & Drains"** in **Blaine, MN**.

Different business name, different city. Citation consistency across directories is a
recognised local ranking factor, and a mismatched name-plus-city pair is one of the more
harmful varieties. Worth a full citation sweep — Nextdoor is the one that surfaced in
search, and where there is one there are usually several.

### G3 — Posting has lapsed (medium)

The most recent post is dated **3 July 2026**, 28 days before this audit. It is a good
post — a real underground copper water-line repair, described concretely, with the service
area named. That is exactly the right kind of content.

The problem is cadence. One post every four weeks is well below the level at which posts
meaningfully contribute, and posts stop displaying prominently as they age. This business
clearly has the raw material: 26 photos on the profile and a steady stream of jobs.

### G4 — The website link is shallow and untagged (medium)

The profile links to the homepage only. Two things follow from that:

1. **No deep links.** A search for "plumber in Coon Rapids" that surfaces the profile
   sends the visitor to a generic homepage rather than to the Coon Rapids page that
   already exists and is 1,870 words long. The business built 74 city pages and the
   profile points at none of them.
2. **No UTM tagging.** There is currently no way to tell how much website traffic comes
   from the Google profile, which means there is no way to measure whether any of this
   work is paying off.

### G5 — Profile differentiators appear nowhere on the website (medium)

The profile publishes three attributes the website never mentions: **Latino-owned**,
**LGBTQ+ friendly**, and by implication **family owned**. It also carries **216 reviews at
4.9**, which the website does not display anywhere.

These are the reasons a customer picks this company over the franchise with a call centre,
and they exist only on a page the customer has already left by the time they are reading
about services.

### G6 — The Blaine address is not reflected on the site (low)

The profile has a verified street address in Blaine. The website publishes no address at
all. For a service-area business, saying where you are dispatched from is a proximity and
trust signal, and it is missing.

## What is already strong

Worth being explicit about, because it changes what is worth paying for:

- **4.9 from 216 reviews** is an excellent profile. Volume *and* rating are both strong.
  Most competitors in this metro would trade places.
- **Open 24 hours** is set correctly and matches the site's 24/7 claim.
- **Booking is enabled.** Many plumbing profiles in the metro have not turned this on.
- **The attributes are populated**, including two that are genuine differentiators.
- **The July post is well written** — specific, jargon-free, names the service area.
  Cadence is the issue, not quality.

This profile does not need rescuing from a bad state. It needs to be connected to the
website and kept warm.
