One Habit at a Time

A weight-loss plan that never asks you to overhaul your life on a Monday. You add one small habit each week and keep the ones you already have. By week twelve you are not on a diet — you have twelve things you just do.

One thing per week

About the scale

Weight moves for a dozen reasons that have nothing to do with what you did — salt, sleep, hormones, a heavy meal the night before. If you weigh daily, you will react to noise. Weigh once a week at the same time, or skip it entirely and track the habits instead. The habits are the thing you control; the weight is a lagging report on them.

When you fall off

You will. A vacation, a bad week, a sick kid. The recovery rule is that you restart at the next meal, not next Monday — and you restart with the habits from weeks 1 through 3, not all twelve. Getting back is a small move, not a fresh start.

This is a behavior plan, not medical advice. If you are pregnant, take medication that affects blood sugar or blood pressure, have a heart or kidney condition, or have any history of disordered eating, talk to your doctor before changing how you eat or move — some of these habits need adjusting for you specifically.
