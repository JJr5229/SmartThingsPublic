Our Story

Black-owned. Family-run. Seasoned right.

Bigg Boyz Kitchen opened its doors on Washington Avenue in 2025 with one simple promise: cook the food our family grew up on, and cook it with love.

That means wangz fried to order and tossed in sauces we make ourselves, catfish with a proper cornmeal crust, greens simmered low and slow with pork, and cakes that never come out of a box. Every soul food plate lands with cornbread on the side — because around here, that's just how it's done.

Whether you're dining in, grabbing carry-out on a lunch break, or feeding the whole block, we serve it bigg.
