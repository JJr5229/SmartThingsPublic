Resources

Where to start, when you do not know where to start

A short, maintained list — Minnesota first, then national. We would rather give you six links that work than sixty you have to sort through at midnight.

Minnesota resources

Gender-affirming healthcare

Children's Minnesota — Gender Health

A pediatric gender health program with support and resources for families navigating care in Minnesota.

childrensmn.org →

Ask us for a referral

Families in our groups have been through most of the clinics in this state. If you tell us where you live and what you need, we can usually point you at someone who has already made that appointment.

Email info@tffmn.org →

Community & youth programs

QUEERSPACE collective

One-to-one mentorship pairing LGBTQ+ youth with LGBTQ+ adult mentors in the Twin Cities.

queerspacecollective.org →

Twin Cities Pride

Year-round programming and the Pride Cultural Arts Center in Minneapolis, where our office is based.

tcpride.org →

Minnesota LGBTQIA2S+ Council

The state's own directory of community resources, services, and advocacy organizations.

mn.gov/lgbtqia2s →

Legal, school & identity documents

Name changes, gender marker updates, school records, and IEP or 504 conversations come up in nearly every parents' group we run. These processes change often enough that a static web page goes stale fast — so we keep the current guidance in the monthly newsletter and in the room.

Ask us directly →   Subscribe to the newsletter →

National resources

For crisis support, research-backed guidance, and organizations working beyond Minnesota.

The Trevor Project

24/7 crisis support

Free, confidential crisis intervention and suicide prevention for LGBTQ+ young people, by phone, text, and chat.

thetrevorproject.org →

Trans Lifeline

Peer crisis line

A trans-run peer support hotline, staffed by trans people, for trans people. No non-consensual active rescue.

translifeline.org →

988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline

24/7 · Call or text 988

Nationwide crisis support. Press 3 for the LGBTQ+ youth subnetwork where available.

988lifeline.org →

PFLAG

The national organization for LGBTQ+ people, their parents, and allies, with local chapters across the country.

pflag.org →

Gender Spectrum

Resources and online groups for gender-expansive youth, parents, caregivers, and the professionals who work with them.

genderspectrum.org →

Family Acceptance Project

Research on how family behaviour affects the health of LGBTQ+ young people — useful when a relative wants evidence, not feelings.

familyproject.sfsu.edu →

Something missing or out of date? Tell us at info@tffmn.org. We review this list quarterly and we would rather hear about a dead link from you than leave it there for the next family.
