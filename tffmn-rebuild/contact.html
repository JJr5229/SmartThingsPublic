The monthly newsletter

This is where the real schedule lives.

Because we do not publish meeting locations on the website, the newsletter is how families get every date, address, room change, weather cancellation, special event, and new group announcement. If you are going to subscribe to one thing, make it this one.

We do not sell, share, or rent the list. Ever. You can unsubscribe from any email in one click.

If you are in crisis right now

Please do not wait for our reply. Call or text 988 for the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline, reach The Trevor Project for LGBTQ+ youth crisis support, or call Trans Lifeline for trans-run peer support. We will still be here afterwards.
