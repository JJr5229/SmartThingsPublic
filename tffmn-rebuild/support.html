Support us

Every meeting is free because someone paid for it first

We do not charge families. Not for the room, not for the snacks, not for the facilitator's training. That only works because people who believe trans kids deserve a soft place to land decide to fund it.

Give now Volunteer instead

Where your gift goes

  • Meeting space across seven communities
  • Facilitator training and support
  • Snacks, supplies, and the kids' room
  • Travel to Greater Minnesota groups
  • Tabling at Pride festivals statewide

Ways to give

Give monthly

A recurring gift is the most useful thing you can do for us. It is what lets us commit to a room in Duluth in March when it is only November.

Set up a monthly gift

Give once

Any amount. $25 covers snacks for a meeting; $150 covers a room for an evening; $500 sends facilitators to a Greater Minnesota group for a season.

Make a one-time gift

Give through GiveMN

Prefer to give through Minnesota's own giving platform, or taking part in Give to the Max Day? We are there too.

At meetings there is a suggested donation of $10 per family or $5 per individual. It is genuinely suggested. Nobody is asked, nobody is tracked, and no family has ever been turned away for not giving.

Other ways to help

Set-up, snacks, greeting new families at the door, or helping in the kids' room. Training provided. Background check required for youth-facing roles.
