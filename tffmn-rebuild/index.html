You are not the only family figuring this out.

Transforming Families runs free, confidential monthly support groups for transgender, non-binary, and questioning youth ages 3–21 — and for the parents, caregivers, and siblings who love them. Across Minnesota, and online.

New here?

Your first meeting

You do not need a referral, a diagnosis, a membership, or any money. You do not have to say anything. You can bring your whole family or come by yourself.

Email info@tffmn.org and we will send you the address, tell you what the room looks like, and answer anything you are nervous about asking.

We reply to every message from a real person, usually within two days.

What we do

Support, all the way across the state

Every group is peer-led. Almost everyone who works or volunteers here is trans, or is the parent of someone who is. We have been where you are standing.

Monthly support groups

In-person meetings in the Twin Cities metro and Greater Minnesota, plus a monthly virtual group so distance is never the reason you cannot come.

Common questions

Before you come

Where are the meetings held?

We do not publish meeting addresses anywhere online. That is deliberate — it protects the privacy and safety of the families who attend. Email info@tffmn.org and we will send you the location, parking notes, and what to expect when you walk in.

How much does it cost?

Nothing. If you are able, there is a suggested donation of $10 per family or $5 per individual at meetings — but it is genuinely suggested. Nobody is turned away, nobody is asked, and nobody keeps track.

My kid is not sure yet. Can we still come?

Yes. Questioning is welcome here, and so is "we do not have the words for this yet." There is no threshold your child has to cross to belong in the room.

I am the one who is struggling, not my kid.

That is exactly what the caregiver groups are for. Parents arrive grieving, scared, angry, or just exhausted — and they meet parents two years further down the road. You are allowed to need support too.

Is it confidential?

Yes. What is said in a meeting stays in the meeting. We do not photograph attendees, we do not publish names, and we do not share our mailing list.

We live outside the Twin Cities.

We hold in-person groups in Greater Minnesota as well, plus a monthly virtual meeting open to families anywhere in the state. See the meeting schedule.

Keep the door open for the next family

Every meeting is free because someone else paid for it first. Your gift keeps rooms rented, facilitators trained, and the lights on for the family who has not found us yet.
