Programs

Everything we run, and who it is for

Most of our programming is intentionally private, to protect the families who take part. Here is what exists, so you know what you are asking about when you write to us.

Peer support groups

Younger kids

Roughly ages 3–10

Play, art, and games in a room where nobody is confused about who they are. Staffed by trained volunteers, many of them trans adults.

Tweens

Roughly ages 10–13

The in-between years, with peers who are in them too. Lower-key than the teen group, more structured than the kids' room.

Teens

Roughly ages 13–21

Peer-facilitated conversation with trans adult facilitators in the room. School, names, families, dating, transition, and everything else.

Siblings

All ages

Being the sibling of a trans kid comes with its own questions, and usually nobody asks you any of them. This group does.

Parents & caregivers

Runs alongside every youth group

A facilitated group to trade resources, ask the questions you cannot ask at work, and hear from families two years further down the road.

Statewide virtual group

Monthly, online

For families anywhere in Minnesota — especially where the nearest in-person group is a long drive away.

Caregivers-only groups

Two groups where no youth are present, because sometimes caregivers need a room of their own.

Parents of adult trans people

Monthly · Minneapolis

When your trans child is grown, the questions change — about relationships, distance, healthcare, and your own role. This group is built for that stage.

Caregivers of trans people, any age

Monthly · Afton

Open to parents, grandparents, guardians, foster and kinship caregivers, and chosen family, regardless of the age of the person you care for.

Beyond the meeting room

Advocacy

We show up for trans rights and protections — at the legislature, in school districts, and alongside partner organizations across Minnesota.

Education & training

Sessions for schools, clinics, faith communities, and workplaces that want to actually get this right rather than guess. Request a training.

Outreach

We table at Pride festivals across the state — often the first place a family finds out we exist, months before they are ready to walk into a meeting.

Day of the Transgender Child

An annual observance now in its tenth year, celebrating trans kids and the families who back them.

Private online community

A closed group for Minnesota families — for the two-in-the-morning questions that cannot wait until the next monthly meeting. Ask about access.

Partner collaboration

We work with clinics, schools, and other LGBTQ+ organizations so families get handed to the right door instead of bounced between them.
