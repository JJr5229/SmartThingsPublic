About us

Built by the families who needed it

Transforming Families is a Minnesota 501(c)(3) nonprofit dedicated to transgender, non-binary, questioning, and intersex youth and the people who support them. Our mission is simple: a better world for trans youth and their families, starting right here in Minnesota.

Peer-led is not a slogan

Almost everyone who works or volunteers with Transforming Families is part of the trans community, or is the parent or caregiver of a trans person. Nobody in the room is studying you. The facilitator running the parents' group has sat in your chair, on a bad night, with the same questions.

That is the whole design. Professional support has its place, and we will happily point you toward it. But there is a specific kind of relief that only comes from a person who has already lived the exact thing you are living, and that is what we exist to provide.

What we have been doing for more than ten years

We started as a handful of families meeting because there was nowhere else to go. More than a decade later, the cornerstone of the work is still the same: monthly in-person and virtual support groups for trans youth and their families throughout Minnesota.

Around that core, the work has grown:

  • Community building — seven in-person groups plus a statewide virtual meeting, and a private online community for families across Minnesota.
  • Advocacy — showing up for trans rights and protections at the capitol, in school boards, and alongside partner organizations.
  • Education and outreach — training, speaking, and tabling at Pride festivals across the state so families find us before they hit a crisis.
  • Day of the Transgender Child — an annual observance we helped establish, now in its tenth year.

Who we serve

Transgender, non-binary, and gender-expansive youth ages 3 to 21, and their parents, caregivers, and siblings. Questioning kids are welcome. Families who are not sure what they think yet are welcome. You do not have to arrive with the right vocabulary or a settled opinion — you only have to arrive.

How we are run

We work out of a small office at the Pride Cultural Arts Center in Minneapolis, run by an Executive Director and a small number of dedicated staff and a great many volunteers. We are a registered 501(c)(3) nonprofit, EIN 99-4006633, and gifts are tax-deductible to the extent allowed by law.

On privacy. We do not publish meeting locations, we do not photograph attendees without explicit consent, and we do not share or sell our mailing list. For many of our families, discretion is not a preference — it is a safety requirement, and we treat it that way.
