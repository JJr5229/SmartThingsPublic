Meetings

Find a group near you

Every group is free, peer-led, and confidential. Come once, come every month, or come and sit quietly in the back until you are ready to talk. All of that is normal here.

Email us for the address

Why there are no addresses on this page. We do not publish meeting locations anywhere public. It is the single most effective thing we do to keep our families safe. Email info@tffmn.org and a human will send you the address, the door to use, and where to park — usually within two days.

What actually happens at a meeting

Most meetings run about two hours. People arrive, sign in with a first name only if they want to, and get a snack. After a short welcome, the room splits into breakout groups so that everyone lands with people their own age or in their own role.

  • Younger kids play, make things, and get to be a kid in a room where nobody is confused about them.
  • Tweens and teens meet separately, with peers and trans adult facilitators.
  • Siblings have their own group — because being the brother or sister of a trans kid comes with its own set of things nobody asks you about.
  • Parents and caregivers meet in a facilitated group to trade resources, vent, ask the questions they cannot ask at work, and hear from families further along.

Then everyone comes back together for announcements and goodbyes. That is it. There is no curriculum, no therapy, no pressure to share, and no cost.
