================================================================================
GLP-1 COMPANION PLANNER — FEATURE LIST
MDRN Milestone Co.  ·  Twelve sections, one file
================================================================================


DASHBOARD
Your week at a glance. A seven-day strip anchored on your dose day shows what has
been logged and what hasn't, alongside a protein ring against your own target,
days since your last dose, current dose strength, your latest non-scale victory,
and a small chart of symptom severity by day since dose.

MY DETAILS
The handful of facts everything else is built on — your name, start date, dose
day of the week, daily protein target, starting and goal weight, and your
prescriber's details. Everything here saves as you type.

DOSE LOG
What you took and when. Each entry records the date, the dose strength, and the
injection site. Rotating your injection site is standard practice, so the site
column is there to save you having to remember which one you used last. The
titration history builds itself as you go.

DAILY LOG  ·  the signature section
Protein first, then everything else. Enter protein by meal — breakfast, lunch,
dinner, snack — and a ring fills toward your daily target as you type. Water and
fibre sit alongside it, and a short row of ticks covers the things that are easy
to lose track of: moved my body, ate slowly, strength session, took my dose. A
free-text note closes out the day. Move between days with the arrows or jump
straight back to today.

HOW IT SAT
Foods change on a GLP-1 — things you used to love can stop agreeing with you, and
it is rarely obvious which. Log a food once, mark it Sat well, Hit or miss, or
Rough, and the planner sorts it into the matching card on its own. Over a few
weeks the pattern shows up without you having to hold it in your head.

SYMPTOMS
Charted against days since your dose rather than against the calendar — that is
the view that actually shows a rhythm. Log a symptom with a severity from 1
(barely there) to 5 (had to stop what I was doing) and the planner works out
which day of your dose cycle it fell on, then averages it into the chart and
tells you in plain language which day tends to be worst.

MEASUREMENTS
Weekly, not daily. Weight moves for a dozen reasons that have nothing to do with
how the week went — and on a GLP-1 it stalls for weeks at a time while everything
else keeps improving. Waist, hips, chest, arm and thigh sit next to weight, with
running totals for inches lost since you started.

STRENGTH
Losing weight costs muscle as well as fat unless you give your body a reason to
keep it; two sessions a week is the usual target. Log the session, what you did
and the loads, and the planner counts your sessions per week against that target.

NON-SCALE VICTORIES
The scale will stall. These are what is actually changing while it does — and on
a hard week they are the record worth rereading. Each entry is dated, and a
rotating prompt is there for the days when nothing comes to mind.

QUESTIONS FOR MY PROVIDER
The thing you meant to ask and forgot the moment you sat down. Write it here when
it occurs to you, tick it off once it has been answered, and print the page before
your appointment.

REFILLS & COSTS
Refill dates, prior-authorisation status, and what this is actually costing you
month to month. The dashboard surfaces the next refill still ahead of you, and a
running total tracks what you have spent so far.

CHECKLIST
Organised by treatment stage rather than by month — people start this at very
different points and move at very different speeds. Covers the first week, the
first month, each titration step, and the longer-term maintenance habits, with
your own items addable at any stage.


--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
ACROSS EVERY SECTION
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

FIVE COLOUR THEMES
Slate & Amber, Clay, Fern, Dusk and Ink. Switch with one click in the sidebar at
any time; two of them are darker themes for evening logging. Your choice is saved
with the rest of your data.

PRINT VIEW
Any single section prints on its own, cleanly, without the navigation or buttons —
useful for taking your dose history or your questions list to an appointment.

ENCRYPTED BACKUPS
Save writes a passphrase-protected backup file to your device using your browser's
own AES-256 encryption. Restore brings it back on any device with the same
passphrase. There is no unencrypted export path, and nothing is ever uploaded
anywhere — the planner has no server behind it.

WORKS OFFLINE, ON ANY DEVICE
One self-contained file. No account, no subscription, no installation. Desktop,
tablet and phone layouts are all built in.
