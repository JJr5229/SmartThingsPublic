GLP-1 Companion Planner Template | Dose Log, Protein Tracker, Symptom & Food
Journal | Editable Web App, Instant Download

A private, week-by-week record of how this is actually going — built around the
things that matter on a GLP-1 and nowhere else. Your dose day anchors everything,
your protein comes first, and the patterns you can never quite remember at your
next appointment are written down while they are still fresh.

This is not a printable and not a spreadsheet. It is a real, self-contained app
that opens in your browser and remembers your data between visits. The dashboard
shows your week at a glance — days logged, protein against your own target, how
many days since your last dose, and a running note of what changed. Every number
you enter updates the charts live.

Twelve sections cover the whole picture. Dose Log tracks each week's injection,
site rotation and titration history. Daily Log puts protein by meal in front of
you with a ring that fills as you go, plus water, fiber and the small daily wins
(moved my body, ate slowly). How It Sat is the section people tell us they wish
they had started sooner — log a food once and the app sorts it into "Sits well,"
"Hit or miss," or "Rough" on its own, so the pattern surfaces without you having
to hold it in your head. Symptoms are charted against days since your dose rather
than against the calendar, which is the view that actually shows a rhythm.
Measurements, Strength, and Non-Scale Victories keep the record honest on the
weeks the scale refuses to move. Questions for My Provider collects the things
you meant to ask, and Refills & Costs tracks what you have paid and when the next
refill is due.

Perfect for anyone starting or already on a GLP-1 who wants to keep their own
records in one calm, private place — and for anyone tired of diet-culture
tracking apps that turn a medical routine into a scoreboard. It also suits people
who want something organized to bring to appointments, or who simply prefer their
health notes on their own device rather than in someone else's account.

HOW IT WORKS
There is no sign-up, no login and no subscription. The planner runs entirely in
your browser and saves to your own device as you type — nothing is uploaded
anywhere, because there is no server behind it to upload to.

To keep a copy or move to another device, use Save. The planner asks you to
choose a passphrase and writes an encrypted backup file to your downloads folder.
Restore on any device, enter the same passphrase, and everything comes back
exactly as you left it. The encryption is done by your own browser (AES-256), so
a backup file that is lost, emailed or left on a shared computer is unreadable
without your passphrase. Choose one you will remember — it cannot be recovered
for you.

GOOD TO KNOW

Q: Do I need an account or a subscription?
A: No. There is nothing to sign up for and nothing to renew. You download one
   file and open it.

Q: Where does my data go?
A: Onto your own device only. The planner has no server and sends nothing
   anywhere. Backups are files you save yourself, and they are encrypted with a
   passphrase you choose.

Q: What if I switch phones or computers?
A: Use Save to write an encrypted backup, move that file across, then use Restore
   and enter your passphrase.

Q: Does it work on my phone?
A: Yes. The layout adapts to phone screens, and there is a menu button for
   navigating between sections.

Q: Can I print it?
A: Yes. Any section can be printed on its own — useful for bringing your dose
   history or your questions list to an appointment.
